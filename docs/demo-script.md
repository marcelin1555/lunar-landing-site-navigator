# Demo video script (30 s maximum, English subtitles)

**Rules:** the video must be **≤ 30 s**, with English subtitles, no people under 18 and a public link. It is a screen recording of the live site only, with no AI-generated imagery.

**Live site:** https://marcelin1555.github.io/lunar-landing-site-navigator/

## Shot list

| # | Time | What is on screen | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 s | Open the **film link** (below): full-screen terrain, the camera orbits slowly and the shadows sweep as the real Sun circles. Record 4 s, then switch to the normal app tab | Where should Artemis land? Sunlight means power. Earth in view means a direct link home. |
| 2 | 4–9 s | Click the **Shackleton Rim** pin; the readout and skyline update | Real terrain from NASA's LOLA, Sun and Earth from SPICE. The real horizon, hour by hour. |
| 3 | 9–14 s | In the tour "Why the south pole is hard", go to step 2 (→) and click **Compute the crater floor**; the skyline turns into a wall | 10 km away, the crater floor: a 20° wall. 0% Sun, 0% Earth in two years. Permanent shadow, where ice hides. |
| 4 | 14–21 s | Open the compare link (Malapert, Shackleton Rim, Nobile Rim 1); hover the table | Landing today? Shackleton Rim gets more Sun overall, but Malapert gives 22 days of Sun *and* Earth vs 13. |
| 5 | 21–26 s | Click **Malapert Massif** in the table, scroll to **Month by month**, click Apr 2028 then Oct 2028 | Launch slips? At Malapert, Sun and Earth together go from 37% of hours (Apr 2028) to 80% (Oct 2028). |
| 6 | 26–30 s | Scroll to the footer (method and validation) | Validated against JPL Horizons (0.005°) and NASA's PGDA maps. Lunar Landing Site Navigator, by Quiral Labs. |

**Film link for shot 1:** https://marcelin1555.github.io/lunar-landing-site-navigator/?film
It hides the panels, starts at the current time, orbits the camera slowly and runs time at about 25 hours per second (one lunar day in about 30 s). Only the key sites keep their names. Reload the page to restart. Wait about 2 s after loading, so the terrain and the time start together, before you begin recording.

## Recording checklist
- [ ] Browser at 1440×900 or larger, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden, no extensions visible.
- [ ] Clear site data first, so no old custom points appear.
- [ ] Before shot 1, set the time to "Now". The compare numbers above hold for 14 Nov 2026; on another day, re-read them from the screen and update the subtitle in shot 4.
- [ ] Record at 30 fps; trim to **≤ 30.0 s**. Check the final duration in the file properties.
- [ ] Burn the subtitles into the video and also upload `demo-subtitles.srt` if the platform accepts it.
- [ ] Upload the video so it plays without login. "Public" visibility is the safest choice; test the link in a private window.
- [ ] Watch it once on a phone: subtitles readable, nothing cut off.

## Fallback: 7 slides
If the video does not fit in 30 s:
1. Title.
2. The problem (Sun = power, Earth = link).
3. Method (LOLA + SPICE → horizon).
4. 3D map.
5. Shackleton floor vs rim.
6. The compare result.
7. Validation and limits.

Use the same subtitle lines as slide text.
